# Seller Helper — Pitch deck script (investor)

**Format:** ~10 slides, **8–10 minutes** speaking time.  
**Tone:** Problem-first, then proof you close the loop (not “another dashboard”).

---

## Slide 1 — Title (30 sec)

**On screen:** Seller Helper · *The decision layer for every e-commerce store* · Vitrina Store

**You say:**

> “Good morning. I’m [Name]. We built **Seller Helper** — not another analytics tool, but the **operations brain** for online sellers.
>
> E-commerce platforms help you **launch** a store in a day. They don’t help you **run** it when traffic is up, conversion is down, and you’re alone behind a phone. Seller Helper fixes that gap — and it’s built to plug into **any** storefront, starting with our own platform, Vitrina Store.”

---

## Slide 2 — The problem (90 sec)

**On screen:** *Too many metrics. Too few actions.*

**You say:**

> “Here’s the pain every e-commerce owner knows.
>
> They install Google Analytics, maybe Hotjar. They get **sessions, devices, bounce rate** — charts that describe **what happened**, not **what to do Monday morning**.
>
> When conversion drops, they ask: *Which product? Which step? Facebook or Google? Fix price, image, or checkout?*
>
> Enterprise tools — Dynamic Yield, Bloomreach — **do** recommend actions, but at **thousands of dollars a month** and for teams with data analysts. **Shopify Magic** gives text advice, but it doesn’t **measure** if the change worked.
>
> The **long tail** of e-commerce — solo sellers, small brands, emerging markets — is left with **dashboard fatigue** and **guesswork**. That’s lost revenue every week, and it’s the problem we solve.”

---

## Slide 3 — Why it matters now (60 sec)

**On screen:** Rising CAC · Thin margins · Mobile-first sellers

**You say:**

> “Three forces make this urgent.
>
> **One:** customer acquisition cost is up — you can’t afford to waste traffic you already paid for on Facebook or Google.
>
> **Two:** sellers run stores from **mobile**, between deliveries — they need **three clear actions**, not fifty metrics.
>
> **Three:** AI made generic advice cheap — but **generic** advice doesn’t move revenue. Sellers need **bounded, reversible actions** tied to **their** catalog and **their** funnel.
>
> The winner in e-commerce tooling isn’t the one with the most charts — it’s the one that **turns behavior into revenue faster than the seller can alone**.”

---

## Slide 4 — Our solution (90 sec)

**On screen:** Observe → Recommend → Apply → Measure

**You say:**

> “Seller Helper is a **decision-support console** inside the store — one page, eight lenses on the same data.
>
> We ingest **privacy-friendly micro-events**: product views, cart, checkout, scroll depth, heatmaps, traffic sources like Google, Facebook, Instagram, security signals — one normalized stream.
>
> Then we don’t dump charts. We run a **closed loop**:
>
> - **Observe** — live funnel, behavior, traffic sources, alerts.
> - **Recommend** — rules plus AI that name a **specific** bottleneck, not ‘improve SEO’.
> - **Apply** — one-click **quick fixes**: default color, promo price, hero review, block a fraudulent session — **no code, no JSON**.
> - **Measure** — every action is logged and appears as a **checkpoint on the Timeline** on the metric it was meant to move.
>
> That loop is what makes us a **problem-solving product**, not a reporting add-on.”

---

## Slide 5 — Product walkthrough (2 min)

**On screen:** 8 tabs — one story

**You say:**

> “Let me walk you through one seller’s week in **60 seconds per tab**.
>
> **Overview** — traffic in the last 24 hours, devices, top pages, active sessions. Pulse check.
>
> **Funnel** — product view → cart → checkout → purchase. Where money leaks, step by step.
>
> **Behavior** — heatmaps, scroll depth, **where traffic comes from** — Google, social, direct. Merchandising and acquisition in one place.
>
> **Vitrina** — per-product fixes: ‘shoppers pick this color — set it as default’, ‘add promo price’, ‘pin your best review on the hero image’. **Catalog intelligence**, not generic tips.
>
> **AI recommendations** — prioritized actions from real signals, with evidence.
>
> **Alerts** — conversion drop, traffic spike, cart abandonment mass, JS errors, slow pages — the five fires every seller cares about.
>
> **Security** — suspicious sessions, block in two clicks.
>
> **Inbox** — recommendations routed by role; implemented or dismissed — **accountability** for the team.
>
> One console. One data layer. **Actions**, not archaeology.”

---

## Slide 6 — Why we win (90 sec)

**On screen:** Comparison table (mental model for investor)

**You say:**

> “Compare us in one sentence each.
>
> **Google Analytics** — describes traffic; doesn’t tell you which SKU to fix.  
> **Hotjar** — shows heatmaps; doesn’t change your product card.  
> **Enterprise personalization** — powerful; **unaffordable** for 95% of stores.  
> **Shopify Magic** — text; **no closed loop**, no per-product merchandising tied to your funnel.
>
> **Seller Helper** combines **behavioral depth**, **AI prioritization**, **one-click reversible fixes**, and **proof on the Timeline** — built for the seller who has **no data team**.
>
> Our moat isn’t the LLM — it’s the **event contract**, the **rule engine**, the **quick-fix layer**, and the **audit trail** that makes recommendations **trustworthy and measurable**.”

---

## Slide 7 — Market & wedge (60 sec)

**On screen:** TAM → SAM → Beachhead

**You say:**

> “Our beachhead is **platform-embedded**: every Vitrina Store merchant gets Seller Helper by default — distribution is **built in**.
>
> The expand path is clear: **Seller Helper as a module** for WooCommerce, Shopify, custom stacks — same loop, same API shape.
>
> TAM is global e-commerce operators; our SAM is **SMB and solo merchants** in MENA and similar mobile-first markets where **French/Arabic ops** and **low technical literacy** make ‘action consoles’ more valuable than BI suites.
>
> We win wedge by **saving one conversion point per month** — that’s often more than any SaaS subscription.”

---

## Slide 8 — Business model (45 sec)

**On screen:** Pricing logic

**You say:**

> “Monetization aligns with value.
>
> **Tier 1 — Included** with platform — basic funnel, alerts, limited AI runs. Drives retention on Vitrina Store.
>
> **Tier 2 — Pro** — unlimited analysis, full Vitrina quick fixes, Timeline checkpoints, team Inbox — **per store / month**.
>
> **Tier 3 — Platform / agency** — multi-store, white-label, API export — for marketplaces and agencies managing dozens of merchants.
>
> We price against **recovered revenue**, not page views — because that’s what the seller feels.”

---

## Slide 9 — Traction & proof (60 sec)

**On screen:** What’s live today (honest)

**You say:**

> “What’s real today: production module on Vitrina Store, **live micro-event pipeline**, deterministic alerts, LLM recommendations with fallback, **four Vitrina quick fixes**, security blocks, French operator UX, graduation-grade documentation and tests.
>
> Early sellers tested on **real catalogs** — that shaped which tabs mattered and which fixes we shipped first.
>
> Next 12 months: **pilot KPI** — time-to-first-action, % recommendations applied, uplift on flagged products 7 days post-checkpoint. That’s the story investors should expect in the next meeting.”

*(Adjust numbers if you have real pilot stats — investors prefer one real metric over many vague claims.)*

## Backup answers (if challenged)

| Question | Short answer |
|----------|----------------|
| “Isn’t this just GA + ChatGPT?” | “GA has no apply layer, no per-SKU Vitrina fixes, no checkpoint Timeline. ChatGPT has no funnel truth or audit trail.” |
| “LLM hallucination?” | “Rules fire first; LLM runs on a **bounded JSON snapshot**; outputs are fingerprinted and idempotent; destructive automation is off by design.” |
| “Privacy?” | “Session-key based telemetry; masked in UI; admin-only surface; reversible actions only.” |
| “Why you?” | “Built on a live store, tested with real merchants, loop already in production — not a slideware prototype.” |

---

## Delivery tips

1. **Open with a story:** “Last week a seller had 400 views and 2 sales — Seller Helper showed cart drop on one product and suggested default color + promo — one click.”
2. **Demo 90 sec** on Funnel → Vitrina quick fix → Timeline checkpoint — stronger than all slides.
3. **Say “problem-solving app” once, then always say “closed loop” — investors remember loops.
4. **End with pilot**, not “we’re pre-revenue” without a next step.

C’est un compagnon de décision, pas un outil d’analyse de plus. »
